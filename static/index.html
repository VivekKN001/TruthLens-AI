    How it works

    Four agents. One honest article.

    Every topic passes through a researcher, a writer, an editor and a fact-checker, all running on your machine, before it reaches you.

    01

    Research

    Four DuckDuckGo searches run in parallel angles: the topic itself, its category, recent research and the latest news. Results are deduplicated and synthesized into a structured brief.

    • ddgs search
    • dedupe by URL
    • retry w/ backoff
    02

    Write

    The writer turns the brief into a full blog post, tuned to the audience and tone of the category you picked, and streams it to you token by token.

    • category voice
    • live streaming
    03

    Edit

    A stricter, lower-temperature editor tightens structure, removes repetition and checks the draft against the research it came from.

    • structure
    • tone
    • accuracy
    04

    Verify

    A final grounding pass hunts for numbers, dates, names and quotes that the research doesn't support. The editor corrects what it finds and the post is checked again, so hallucinated specifics get fixed, not published.

    • grounding check
    • auto-correct
    • re-check
